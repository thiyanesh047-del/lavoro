Lavoro uses Claude, through your own Claude plan or your own API key, to score roles, read pages and draft emails. AI output can be wrong, out of date, or made up. Fit scores are estimates, not judgements. Read and check every fact, name and claim in a draft before you send it. You are responsible for anything you send, whoever or whatever wrote the first version.

The person it names may have changed jobs, and an email address may be a guess based on the company's usual pattern. We make no promise that any listing, person, address or description is correct, current or complete.

Privacy

We collect nothing. Lavoro has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry. Its site runs only on your own computer, at 127.0.0.1, and does not answer requests from other machines.

What stays on your computer: your config.yaml, profile.md, the database in data/, the files in output/, and, if you connect Gmail, token.json. These hold your personal data and that of the people Lavoro finds. Keep them safe and never upload them.

What leaves your computer, and only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Anthropic (Claude), through the claude program and your own plan or API key: your profile, job ads, and company and contact details, to score roles, run web searches and draft emails. Anthropic's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Job boards and Platsbanken: requests for their public job lists. Your profile is not sent.
  • Company websites, Wikipedia and Google's public DNS: company names and web addresses, to describe a company and to check that its email domain exists.
  • Hunter.io, only if you add your own key: a company's domain and the names of the people Lavoro is looking for.
  • Gmail, only if you turn it on: the drafts Lavoro writes go into your own Drafts folder. The permission it asks for (gmail.compose) creates drafts and cannot read your inbox. Lavoro never sends.

Your data and other people's: because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. You control all of it: deleting the data/ and output/ folders removes everything Lavoro saved. If someone asks you what you hold about them or asks you to delete it, that request is yours to answer.
